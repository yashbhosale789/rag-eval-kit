Caring for houseplants

Most houseplants die from too much water, not too little. Water only when the top two centimetres of soil feel dry to the touch.

A snake plant tolerates low light and can go three to four weeks between waterings in winter.

Repot a plant when roots grow out of the drainage holes, usually every one to two years. Choose a new pot only two to five centimetres wider than the old one.

Feed flowering plants with a liquid fertiliser every two weeks during spring and summer, and stop feeding in winter.
