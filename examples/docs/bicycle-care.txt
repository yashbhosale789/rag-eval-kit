Bicycle care basics

Check tyre pressure once a week. Road tyres usually run between 80 and 100 psi, while mountain bike tyres run between 25 and 35 psi. The recommended range is printed on the side of the tyre.

Clean and lubricate the chain every 300 kilometres, or after any ride in the rain. Wipe the chain with a dry rag, apply one drop of lubricant to each roller, then wipe off the excess.

Brake pads wear down over time. Replace rim brake pads when the grooves on the pad surface are no longer visible.

Store the bicycle indoors if possible. Leaving it outside in wet weather makes the chain and cables rust quickly.
